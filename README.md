**Problem Statement**

Booking movie or event tickets for a group is often inconvenient because one person has to coordinate ticket selection, collect payments and complete the entire booking.

This creates unnecessary financial responsibility for the organiser and makes group bookings difficult to manage.

**Our Proposed Solution**

We propose introducing GroupBook, a collaborative booking feature that allows users to create groups, invite friends, select movie or event  then autoamttcially best perfered seats are alloted but if the user want to change he can , then during payment the final amount is dived into no of members in the group and on each member book my show a qr code is generated spillting the amount equally  

Each group member can independently complete their payment. Once payment is successful, their ticket gets confirmed. The system also supports partial group bookings, allowing members who have successfully paid to receive their tickets even if other members do not complete their payments.

**Key Innovation**

Unlike conventional group bookings, GroupBook distributes the payment responsibility among individual members while keeping the entire booking process connected through one shared group.

**Expected Impact**

* Simplifies group movie and event bookings.
* Eliminates the need for one person to pay for everyone.
* Makes payment tracking transparent.
* Supports independent ticket confirmation.
* Reduces coordination difficulties.
